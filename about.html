Founder

branding, marketing, startup acceleration, and business growth.

Petra is the Founder of Naru Landing, where she helps innovative Korean companies successfully enter and grow in the North American market. With a background spanning branding, marketing, startup acceleration, and business expansion, Petra combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution to help businesses navigate commercialization, market entry, and long-term growth.

Before founding Naru Landing, she led branding and digital projects for startups and global organizations, working across brand identity, investor communications, marketing systems, websites, and go-to-market strategy. Her experience working with high-growth companies and enterprise clients has shaped a practical approach that connects business strategy with strong brand execution.

Today, Petra partners with founders, innovation organizations, and technology companies to build meaningful market presence through localization, strategic partnerships, and sustainable growth across Canada and the United States.
