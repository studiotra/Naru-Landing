Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Naru Landing (“Naru,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, subscribe to updates, or contact us about our consulting and growth services.

Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email address, phone number, company, and job title
  • Inquiry content — messages you send via forms, email, or calls
  • Newsletter data — email address when you subscribe
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, cookies, and usage data

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule discovery calls
  • Provide services, insights, and updates you request or consent to receive
  • Improve our website, communications, and client experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse

Legal basis

Depending on your location, we process personal information based on consent, performance of a contract, legitimate interests, or legal requirements under applicable laws, including Canada’s PIPEDA where relevant.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers (hosting, email, analytics) who assist our operations under confidentiality obligations, with authorities when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer where the recipient agrees to protect your data.

International transfers

We operate in Canada and South Korea. Your information may be processed in either country and where our service providers are located, with appropriate safeguards where required.

Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. Contact us at info@narulanding.com to exercise these rights.

Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies for site functionality and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites (for example, LinkedIn). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

Children

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
